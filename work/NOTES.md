# Temporary phase notes

Keep only new, unresolved discoveries here while a phase is active. At the
phase boundary—or earlier when a verified fact changes a safety/tool decision:

- move current operational decisions to `PLAN.md`;
- promote reusable evidence to the detected engine profile `engines/<engine>.md`;
- remove resolved temporary notes after confirming their destination.

Record portable resource-path patterns during discovery, not every individual
asset filename. Do not duplicate one finding into every destination or rewrite
unchanged findings after each batch.
