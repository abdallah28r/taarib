This sample supports the `KEEP` treatment for embedded character-design text,
the ban on invented panels/overlays, and direct side-by-side visual review.
Automated dimension, hash, and pixel-boundary checks remain necessary but cannot
make an aesthetic or semantic approval decision. The review manifest must be a
record of the agent's real inspection, not a way to manufacture that inspection.
