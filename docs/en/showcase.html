Follow-up pages

Real screenshots of the template's pages, with data from an invented game, “Station Kepler”: no text or art from a real game.

The text and image pages stay on your computer and are never uploaded, because they hold the game's content.

Dashboard dashboard.html

What waits for you on top, then the phase and its gate, recent events, the last game probe, progress, proofreading quality, and the plan.

Task board tasks.html

Trello-like columns: later, next, doing, waiting on you, verify, done. Each card keeps its dated history, and no task closes without evidence.

Text before and after texts.html

Every line with its translation, status, speaker, problem codes and proofreading notes, with search and filters.

Images before and after images.html

Source and localized image with a comparison slider, and every version tried with its verdict. Spoilers stay blurred until clicked.
