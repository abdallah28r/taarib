# Legal policy

This is working guidance for this project, not legal advice.

## Scope

Taarib is for games you made, games whose own language files or documented mod
system allow the work, and games whose rights holder gave written permission.

It does not decrypt or unscramble game data, read keys, bypass DRM, anti-cheat,
signatures, or access controls, decompile games, add code to a game, or patch an
executable. It is not for online games.

## Copying from other projects

Do not copy code, prose, prompts, artwork, fonts, data, or binaries from another
project unless its license allows that use. Keep the required notices in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. If the license is unclear, do not copy.

## Publishing

Never publish game files, extracted game text, game art, keys, or fonts without the
right to do so. A shareable game patch needs `release-clearance.yaml` and the rights
holder's permission (`references/release.md`). That record is not legal clearance.

## Reports from rights holders

See "Rights holders" in `README.md`. Remove reported material first when the report
is credible, then investigate. Keep any report and do not delete related records.
